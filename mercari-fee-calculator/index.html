How Mercari fees work in 2026

Selling fee. Since January 6, 2025, Mercari charges sellers a flat 10% of the item price plus any shipping the buyer pays, on new and updated listings. The fee comes out of your sale, so you never pay it separately.

No processing fee. Listings created or updated since that date carry no separate payment processing fee. The old 2.9% + $0.50 processing fee belongs to the previous fee structure, so it isn't in this calculator.

Buyer Protection fee. The buyer pays 3.6% of the item price plus buyer-paid shipping at checkout. It doesn't come out of your earnings, but it does raise the total your buyer sees, which is why the calculator shows it.

Cashing out. Direct Deposit to your bank is free. A deposit that fails costs $2. Instant Pay sends money to an eligible debit card in about 30 minutes for $3 per cash-out, up to $600 a month and once a day.

Buyer-paid shipping vs free shipping

Buyer pays shipping: you earn = price - 10% x (price + shipping)
Free shipping: you earn = price - 10% x price - label

Worked example: you sell a $40 item that cost you $12, and Mercari's label is $8.

Buyer pays shipping. The fee is 10% of $48, which is $4.80. You earn $35.20 and your profit is $23.20. The buyer pays $48 plus a $1.73 Buyer Protection fee, $49.73 before sales tax.

Free shipping at the same $40. The fee drops to $4.00, but the $8.00 label comes out of your sale. You earn $28.00 and your profit is $16.00. The buyer pays $40 plus $1.44, which is $41.44.

Free shipping priced at $48. The fee is $4.80, the label is $8.00 and you earn $35.20, exactly the same as buyer-paid shipping. The buyer also pays the same $49.73. So free shipping doesn't cost you more in fees. It only costs you more if you don't add the label to your price.

If you cash out that buyer-paid sale on its own with Instant Pay, your profit drops by $3.00 to $20.20.

Pricing backward from what you want to earn

Buyer pays shipping: price = (target + 10% x shipping) / 0.9
Free shipping: price = (target + label) / 0.9

To earn $30 with an $8 label, list at $34.22 with buyer-paid shipping: the fee is 10% of $42.22, which is $4.22, and $34.22 - $4.22 = $30.00. With free shipping, list at $42.22: the fee is $4.22, the label is $8.00 and you keep $30.00. The buyer pays $43.74 before sales tax either way. If you accept offers, list a little above this so an accepted offer still earns what you need.

Other costs to know

Instant Pay is per cash-out, not per sale. $3 on a single sale that earns $35.20 is about 8.5% of it. Let a few sales build up, or use free Direct Deposit when you can wait.

Cheap items with free shipping. A $10 item with an $8 free-shipping label earns $1.00 after the $1.00 fee and the label. If the item cost you $5, you lose $4.00. Let the buyer pay shipping on small items, or bundle them.

Cancellations. If you cancel sales often, Mercari warns you first and then may charge a cancellation fee of 5% of the item price, up to $25.

Label problems. If a package is heavier or bigger than the label you bought, Mercari takes the overweight or oversize charge from your sales. USPS charges $50 when a non-hazardous item ships in a box that still has a visible HAZMAT label. For packages postmarked on or after September 1, 2026, Mercari splits that fee with you, so $25 comes out of your earnings.

Authentication. Mercari's fee page lists a $5 Mercari Authenticate fee, paid by the seller, for items over a set price that go through third-party authentication, and $10 for an Authenticate Certificate.

Questions

How much does Mercari take from a $40 sale?

10%, which is $4.00, if you offer free shipping. If the buyer pays $8 shipping, the fee is $4.80 because it also applies to that shipping, and you earn $35.20.

Does Mercari charge its 10% fee on shipping?

Yes, on shipping the buyer pays. Mercari's fee page sets the fee at 10% of the item price plus buyer-paid shipping, so with free shipping the fee is on the item price only and the label is taken out of your earnings instead.

Is free shipping or buyer-paid shipping better on Mercari?

They earn the same if you add the label to your price. A $48 item with free shipping and a $40 item with $8 buyer-paid shipping both earn $35.20, and the buyer pays $49.73 either way. Free shipping only earns less if you don't raise the price.

Does Mercari still charge a payment processing fee or a fee to cash out?

There is no separate processing fee on listings created or updated since January 6, 2025. Direct Deposit is free, with a $2 fee only if a deposit fails. Instant Pay costs $3 per cash-out, up to $600 a month.
