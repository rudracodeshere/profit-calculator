# Sample delivery: Etsy profit and pricing check

*Portfolio sample using a demo shop. Fees: Etsy US rates (6.5% transaction, 3% + $0.25 payment processing, $0.20 listing fee per sale).*

## Summary
1 of 4 products loses money on every sale, and 2 more sit below your 30% target. The fixes below would lift monthly profit from $31.81 to about $130 if sales volume held (it may dip after a price rise, so treat that as the ceiling and re-check in 2 weeks).

## Why the mug loses money
Price $27.75 with free shipping. Clay, glaze and kiln share $12.50, box and padding $2.80, shipping label $15.11, Etsy fees $3.08. Total $33.49, so every mug sold costs you $5.74. The shipping label alone eats 54% of the price.

## Recommended prices (30% margin target)
Formula: price = (cost + $0.45 fixed fees) ÷ (1 − 9.5% fees − 30% margin). "Cost x 2" misleads because fees grow with the price.

| Product | Current | Recommended | Profit per unit after |
|---|---|---|---|
| Handmade Ceramic Mug | $27.75, free shipping | Keep $27.75, charge $16.70 shipping | $9.36 (was -$5.74) |
| Lavender Soy Candle 8oz | $17.62 | $20.75 | $6.23 (was $3.39) |
| Linen Tote Bag | $24.00 | $28.75 | $8.60 (was $4.30) |
| Frog Sticker Pack (5) | $6.69 | keep | already 43% |

Mug option B: keep free shipping and price it at $51. That is likely too high for the market, so option A (charge shipping) is the recommendation.
