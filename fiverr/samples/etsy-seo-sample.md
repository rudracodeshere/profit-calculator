# Sample delivery: Etsy SEO rewrite (2 listings)

*Portfolio sample. The shop and listings are made up to show the format a buyer receives.*

## Listing 1: Lavender soy candle

**Old title**
Handmade Candle - Lavender - Relaxing - Gift - Soy Wax - Home Decor - Candles - Aromatherapy Candle Gift for Her

**New title**
Lavender Soy Candle 8oz, Hand Poured Aromatherapy Candle, Relaxing Gift for Her

Why: the old title repeats "candle" three times and leads with the vaguest word. The new one opens with the exact phrase buyers type ("lavender soy candle"), adds size (a top buyer question), and keeps the gift angle once.

**Tags (13, each under 20 characters)**
| # | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | candle | lavender candle |
| 2 | candles | soy candle |
| 3 | handmade | aromatherapy candle |
| 4 | gift | relaxing gift |
| 5 | lavender | self care gift |
| 6 | soy | gift for her |
| 7 | home decor | calming candle |
| 8 | relax | hand poured candle |
| 9 | aromatherapy | natural soy candle |
| 10 | (empty) | bedtime candle |
| 11 | (empty) | spa candle |
| 12 | (empty) | stress relief gift |
| 13 | (empty) | candle gift box |

Why: 3 tags were blank and 6 were single words that match millions of listings. Every new tag is a 2 to 3 word phrase a buyer would search.

**New description (first lines are what shows in search previews)**
> Hand poured 8oz lavender soy candle that fills a room with a soft, calming scent. Made with 100% soy wax, a cotton wick and pure lavender essential oil.
>
> - Size: 8oz jar, about 40 hours burn time
> - Scent: lavender essential oil, not synthetic fragrance
> - Ships in a gift box, ready to give
>
> Perfect for bedtime, a bath, or a self care gift for a friend, mum or coworker.
>
> Care: trim the wick to 1/4 inch before each burn and burn for 2 to 3 hours at a time for an even melt.

## Listing 2: Frog sticker pack

**Old title:** Cute stickers
**New title:** Cute Frog Sticker Pack of 5, Waterproof Vinyl Stickers for Water Bottle and Laptop

**Tags:** frog stickers, frog sticker pack, cute frog sticker, vinyl stickers, waterproof sticker, laptop stickers, water bottle decal, cottagecore sticker, kawaii stickers, frog lover gift, sticker bundle, journal stickers, small gift for kids

Why: "cute stickers" gives Etsy almost nothing to match. Buyers search by the subject (frog), the material (vinyl, waterproof) and where it goes (laptop, water bottle).
