**Description**
```
Months open, a handful of views, zero or almost zero sales? You don't need more guesses. You need someone to go through the shop the way a buyer and Etsy search do, and tell you plainly what is stopping sales.

What I check:
- Titles and tags: do they match what buyers type, or waste slots on repeats and single words?
- First photo and thumbnail: can a buyer tell what it is, how big it is, and why it's different?
- Pricing: does each sale still make money after Etsy fees, shipping and materials?
- Descriptions, shipping times and policies: do they answer the questions that stop buyers ordering?
- Shop basics: banner, About section, sections, reviews and listing count

You get a written report with every problem ranked by impact, the exact fix for each, and what to change first. No vague "improve your SEO" advice.

Standard and Premium also include rewritten titles and tags (and descriptions in Premium) ready to paste.

Send your shop name and I'll confirm which package fits before you order.
```

**FAQ**
1. Q: Do you need my Etsy login? A: No. I only use what buyers can see plus the stats you choose to share. Never send your password.
2. Q: Can you guarantee sales? A: No one honestly can. I find the problems that are clearly costing you sales and tell you how to fix them.
3. Q: Do you use AI tools? A: Yes, for keyword ideas, fee math and first drafts. Every finding is checked against your actual shop and edited by hand.
4. Q: My shop is brand new. Is this worth it? A: Yes. Fixing titles, tags, photos and prices before you have traffic means the first visitors see a shop that can sell.
