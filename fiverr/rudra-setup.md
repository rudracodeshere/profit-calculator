# Rudra's step: open the Fiverr shop (about 30 to 40 minutes, once)

Files are in your Downloads folder under `fiverr/` (gig images, samples). All text is in `gigs.md` and `profile.md`, copy and paste only.

1. Go to fiverr.com > **Become a Seller**. Sign up with the same Gmail you use for Gumroad. One account only (Fiverr bans duplicates). You must be 18+.
2. Fill the profile from `profile.md`: name, photo, description, languages, skills. Skip "Certifications" and "Education" if you want.
3. **Verify:** phone number by SMS, and ID verification if Fiverr asks (government ID, sometimes a selfie check). This is private, never shown to buyers.
4. Create **Gig 1** (Etsy SEO) from `gigs.md`: title, category, tags, the 3 packages, description, FAQ, requirements, and upload `gig1-etsy-seo.png`. Click **Publish**.
5. Same for **Gig 2** and **Gig 3**. If Fiverr only lets you publish one or two at first, publish Gig 1 and 2.
6. Payouts: Settings > Payments. Connect PayPal (same one you'll use for Gumroad) when you set it up. Not needed before the first order; earnings wait for you.
7. Turn on Fiverr app notifications on your phone so buyer messages don't sit unanswered (response time affects ranking).
8. Paste the three gig links in this thread.

If the gig editor rejects a field (category name changed, a word not allowed), paste the error here and I'll fix the text.

## After you're live: how an order works
1. A buyer messages or orders. You paste their message and attached files into this thread.
2. I write the reply or do the work and give you the finished file plus the delivery message.
3. You send it on Fiverr with **Deliver Now**. A few minutes of your time per order.

Never share an email, phone, link, or payment outside Fiverr in chat; Fiverr bans for it. If a buyer asks, reply "Fiverr only lets us talk here."
