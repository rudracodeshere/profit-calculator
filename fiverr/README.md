# Track 1: fast cash from text-only services (Fiverr)

Started 2026-10-13. Owner: Claude does all the work; Rudra signs up, verifies, and clicks Deliver.

## Why Fiverr, why these 3 gigs
- **Fiverr over Upwork:** listing gigs is free. Upwork charges "Connects" to bid on every job, which breaks our no-upfront-money rule.
- **Etsy niche:** we already built Etsy fee/profit tooling (sheet + calculator), so the profit gig is something most Fiverr sellers can't copy quickly. Etsy SEO is the high-volume entry gig (5,900+ competing gigs, entry prices $10 to $15, typical $50 for 5 listings), so we price low to get the first reviews.
- **Spreadsheets:** broad demand beyond Etsy; our build_sheet.py shows Claude can produce working formula workbooks.

| Gig | Basic / Standard / Premium | Our cost per order |
|---|---|---|
| Etsy SEO titles, tags, descriptions | $10 / $25 / $50 | ~5 min of Rudra's time |
| Etsy profit and pricing check | $15 / $35 / $70 | ~5 min |
| Custom Excel / Sheets tracker | $15 / $40 / $80 | ~5 min |

Fiverr keeps 20%. A $10 order pays $8, then PayPal takes ~4.4% + fixed fee + FX on withdrawal. Keep the e-FIRA/FIRC record for each payout (Indian FEMA rules).

## Honest expectations
New Fiverr sellers commonly wait weeks for a first order; the forum is full of "0 orders" posts, and Fiverr can reject a new seller profile. Low entry prices, 1-day delivery, fast replies and a sharp niche are the levers we control. This is fast compared with SEO, not instant.

## Metric and kill rule
- **Goal:** first paid order by **2026-10-27** (2 weeks after gigs go live).
- **Oct 27 check:** gig impressions under ~300 = visibility problem (rewrite titles/tags, add gigs 4 to 7). Impressions fine but 0 orders = offer problem (cut Basic to $5, sharpen images).
- **2026-11-10:** still $0 from Fiverr = stop and move the effort elsewhere.
- After 5 delivered orders with 5-star reviews, raise prices by ~50%.

## Rules we follow
Fiverr ToS: no contact info, links or off-platform payment; one account; AI use disclosed in every gig FAQ; no fake or traded reviews; deliver original work for each buyer.
