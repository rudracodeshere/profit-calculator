How Fiverr fees work

The seller's 20%. Fiverr keeps 20% of everything a buyer pays you: the gig package, Gig Extras and tips. You earn the other 80%. If you give a buyer a Seller Plus coupon or promotion, you earn 80% of the discounted price, so a 10% discount cuts your earnings by 10% too.

The buyer's service fee. On top of your price, the buyer pays a service fee of 5.5%, plus $3.50 when the payment is under $200. Each payment is charged on its own: the order, an extra added after the order started, and a tip each get their own 5.5% fee, and their own $3.50 if that payment is under $200. That is why a $10 tip costs the buyer $14.05.

Withdrawal and early payout. Fiverr charges nothing to withdraw to PayPal (minimum $1). Through Payoneer, a USD wire to your bank costs $1, a local-currency bank transfer $3 (minimum $20 for either), and a transfer to a Payoneer account $3 (minimum $10). The fee is per withdrawal, not per order, and you can withdraw up to $5,000 at a time. Top Rated sellers and Seller Plus Premium members who meet Fiverr's eligibility criteria can use early payout to clear earnings sooner, for 1% of the earnings cleared.

Clearing. Earnings from a completed order clear over 14 days before you can withdraw them, and a tip clears on its own 14-day clock. Top Rated sellers wait 7 days, and Fiverr Pro sellers also get a shorter clearing period.

The formula

Buyer service fee = payment x 5.5% + $3.50 if that payment is under $200

You receive = (gig + extras + tip) x 80% - early payout fee - withdrawal fee

Gig price for a take-home target = (target + withdrawal fee) / 0.8 (or / 0.792 with early payout)

Worked example

A $50 gig, no tip, withdrawn to PayPal. The buyer's service fee is $2.75 (5.5% of $50) plus the $3.50 small order fee, $6.25 in all, so the buyer pays $56.25. Fiverr's 20% commission is $10.00 and you receive $40.00. Fiverr keeps $16.25 altogether, 28.9% of what the buyer paid, and the buyer's fee alone adds 12.5% to your price.

The buyer then tips $10. The tip is a separate payment with its own fee: $0.55 plus $3.50, so $4.05. The buyer has now paid $70.30, you receive $48.00 and Fiverr keeps $22.30 (31.7%).

If instead the buyer adds a $20 extra after the order started, that is its own payment too, with a $4.60 fee ($1.10 + $3.50), and the buyer pays $80.85 in all. Had the extra been picked at checkout as part of one $70 order, the buyer's fees would have been $7.35 instead of $10.85.

A $250 gig withdrawn to a Payoneer account ($3) with early payout: the buyer fee is $13.75 with no small order fee, so the buyer pays $263.75. Your 80% is $200.00, early payout takes $2.00 and the withdrawal $3.00, leaving $195.00.

Working backwards: to take home $100 from one order via PayPal without early payout, price the gig at $100 / 0.8 = $125.00.

Pricing tips

Start from what you want to keep and divide by 0.8. Then look at the buyer's side. Under $200 the $3.50 small order fee makes cheap gigs look expensive at checkout: a $10 gig costs the buyer $14.05, 40.5% more than your price, while a $200 gig costs $211.00, just 5.5% more. Packages that bundle the extras buyers usually want keep everything in one payment with one fee.

Coupons and promotions come out of your 80%, so a 10% coupon on a $50 gig cuts your earnings from $40.00 to $36.00. If your payout method charges a fee, let your balance build up and withdraw several orders at once.

Questions

How much does Fiverr take from sellers?

20% of every order, including Gig Extras and tips, so you earn 80%. A $50 gig pays you $40.00. Withdrawing to PayPal is free, while Payoneer charges $1 to $3 per withdrawal.

What service fee does the buyer pay on Fiverr?

5.5% of each payment, plus $3.50 on payments under $200. A $50 order costs the buyer $56.25 and a $200 order $211.00. Fiverr's Payment Terms (updated September 2026) confirm these rates, but one help article still says $3.00 on orders under $100, so check the fee shown at checkout and edit the rates above if it differs.

Does Fiverr take a cut of tips?

Yes. You earn 80% of a tip, and the buyer pays the standard service fee on it as a separate payment. A $10 tip costs the buyer $14.05 and pays you $8.00. The minimum tip is $5.

How much does it cost to withdraw money from Fiverr?

PayPal is free (minimum $1). Bank transfer via Payoneer is $1 for a USD wire or $3 in local currency (minimum $20), and a Payoneer account is $3 (minimum $10). You can withdraw up to $5,000 at a time once earnings clear, after 14 days (7 for Top Rated sellers). Early payout, for Top Rated and Seller Plus Premium sellers, costs 1% of the earnings.
