How Depop's fees work in 2026

If you are based in the US, UK or Australia, Depop takes no selling fee. You still pay up to three things on each sale:

Payment processing. 3.3% + $0.45 in the US, 2.9% + £0.30 in the UK and 2.6% + A$0.30 in Australia. Depop's fee page says Depop Payments (via Stripe) charges it on the item sale price plus shipping costs and any applicable taxes. That is why the fee looks bigger than 3.3% of your price.

Boosting fee. 12% in the US and UK for listings created from 23 March 2026, and 8% in Australia. You pay it only when a buyer viewed, clicked or liked the boosted listing and bought within 28 days. It is charged on the item price excluding tax, plus the shipping cost only if you arrange your own shipping. It is not charged on Depop Shipping labels.

Shipping. With a Depop Shipping label you are paid the item price plus shipping, and the label cost is deducted along with the fees. If you arrange your own shipping, you keep the shipping money and pay the postage yourself.

Processing = (item + shipping + tax) x 3.3% + $0.45
Boost = item x 12%, or (item + shipping) x 12% if you ship yourself
Profit = item + shipping - processing - boost - label or postage - item cost

These rules come from Depop's Seller fees and charges, Boosted Listings and I don't recognise a fee or charge help pages. If a sale is refunded, Depop reverses all of its fees, boost included.

What changed and when

  • UK: the 10% selling fee was removed for listings created from 20 March 2024. Buyers started paying a Marketplace fee on 15 April 2024.
  • US: the 10% selling fee was removed for new listings on 15 July 2024. The buyer Marketplace fee started on 18 July 2024.
  • Boosting: the fee page lists 12% for new US and UK listings from 23 March 2026, and 8% in Australia. It does not give a rate for US or UK listings created before that date, and one Depop help page (I don't recognise a fee or charge) still says 8%. Your receipt shows the boosting fee charged, so if it differs, enter that rate under Edit fee rates.
  • Australia: the selling fee was removed for sellers based in Australia from 22 July 2026, and buyers there started paying a Marketplace fee the same day.
  • eBay: eBay completed its purchase of Depop on 30 July 2026. Depop's eBay FAQ (updated 30 July 2026) says there are no planned changes to fees or payments at this time.

Worked example

A US seller sells a jacket for $40. The buyer pays $6 shipping, the Depop Shipping label costs $6, there is no sales tax, the jacket cost $10, and the sale did not come from a boost.

  • Processing: 3.3% x $46.00 = $1.518, plus $0.45 = $1.97.
  • Paid to your Depop Balance: $46.00 - $1.97 - $6.00 label = $38.03.
  • Profit after the $10 cost: $28.03. Depop's fees are 4.9% of the item price.

Change one thing at a time and the numbers move like this:

  • The sale came from a boost, Depop label: boost 12% x $40 = $4.80, profit $23.23.
  • Boosted, but you arrange your own shipping: boost 12% x $46 = $5.52, $38.51 paid to your balance, profit $22.51 after $6 postage.
  • Same again with 8% sales tax ($3.20) in the buyer's state: processing is charged on $49.20, so it is $2.07, while the boost stays $5.52 because tax is excluded. Profit $22.41.
  • The first sale in the UK (£40, £6 shipping, £6 label, £10 cost): 2.9% x £46 + £0.30 = £1.63, profit £28.37.
  • To make $25 profit with the first setup, list at $36.86, or $42.09 if the sale comes from a boost.

Depop's own Boosted Listings Policy gives this example: a boosted item that sells for £50 including shipping pays £6.00 boosting and £2.10 processing, £8.10 in total. Its processing figure uses 3.3% + £0.45, the US rates, not the UK rate on the fee page. Enter a $50 price, $0 shipping and own shipping with the boost ticked and the calculator gives the same $8.10 at US rates.

When boosting is worth it

At 12%, a boost costs $4.80 on a $40 item, more than twice the processing fee, and $5.52 if you ship yourself, because then shipping is boosted too. You only pay when the buyer saw or interacted with the boosted listing and bought within 28 days. So the real question is whether the boost brings a sale you would not have made in that time. It tends to make sense for slow movers and items with margin to spare, and less sense for items that sell fast anyway or that you have already priced tight. If you boost, using a Depop Shipping label keeps shipping out of the boost fee.

Cheap items and the fixed fee

The fixed $0.45 is charged once per order, so it hits small sales hardest. A $2 item with free shipping pays $0.52 in processing, 26.0% of the price, leaving $1.48 before you pay for postage. Depop's fee page says processing applies once per bundle transaction, so bundling cheap pieces or setting a minimum price for small items keeps more of each sale.

Questions

Does Depop still charge a 10% selling fee?

Not for sellers based in the US, UK or Australia. The UK dropped it for listings created from 20 March 2024, the US for new listings from 15 July 2024, and Australia from 22 July 2026. Sellers elsewhere still pay 10% on the item price, plus shipping if they don't use a Depop label, and PayPal's processing fee on top.

Why is my Depop processing fee more than 3.3% + $0.45 of my price?

Because it is charged on the item price plus shipping plus any sales tax, not on the price alone. A $40 item with $6 shipping pays 3.3% of $46.00 plus $0.45, which is $1.97. Add 8% sales tax and the base becomes $49.20, so the fee is $2.07. On a $2 item with free shipping, the $0.52 fee is 26% of the price.

How much is the Depop boosting fee and when do I pay it?

12% in the US and UK for listings created from 23 March 2026, and 8% in Australia. You pay it only if the buyer viewed, clicked or liked the boosted listing and bought within 28 days. It is charged on the item price excluding tax, plus shipping only if you arrange your own shipping, and never on a Depop Shipping label. On a $40 item that is $4.80, and it is reversed if the sale is refunded.

Does the buyer's Marketplace fee come out of my payout?

No. Buyers pay it on top of the price you set: up to 5% of the item price plus a fixed amount of up to $1, £1 or A$1, not counting tax and shipping. In the US and UK the exact amount is shown at checkout; in Australia it is built into the price buyers see. Your payout is the item price plus shipping, minus processing, any boost fee and any Depop label cost.
