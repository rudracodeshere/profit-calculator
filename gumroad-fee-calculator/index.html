Two fee schedules, depending on where the buyer came from

Direct sales. When a buyer comes through your own link, your profile, an embed or your email list, Gumroad charges 10% of the price plus $0.50. Third-party summaries of Gumroad's pricing report that card processing, roughly 2.9% + $0.30, is taken on top of that. Check Gumroad's pricing page for the current terms; every rate here is editable.

Discover sales. When the buyer finds you through Gumroad's own marketplace, Gumroad takes a flat 30%, which already covers processing.

Direct fees = price x 12.9% + $0.80  |  Discover fees = price x 30%

Example: a $10 template. A direct sale costs $1.50 in Gumroad fees and $0.59 in processing, $2.09 in total or 20.9%, so you keep $7.91. A Discover sale costs $3.00 and you keep $7.00.

Where the fixed $0.50 starts to matter

The 30% Discover fee sounds expensive, but it has no fixed part. A direct sale carries $0.80 of fixed fees ($0.50 to Gumroad and $0.30 to processing) whatever the price. At $10 that fixed part is 8.0% of the price. Below $8.00 it is more than 10%.

Below $4.68, a Discover sale actually costs you less than a direct sale. On a $3 product, a direct sale keeps $1.81 (fees take 39.6%) while a Discover sale keeps $2.10. On a $1 product a direct sale keeps just $0.07, against $0.70 from Discover.

If you sell low-priced items through your own audience, bundling several into one product, or setting a pay-what-you-want minimum around $5, keeps the fixed fee from swallowing each sale.

Questions

How much does Gumroad take from a $10 sale?

On a direct sale, $1.50 in Gumroad fees (10% + $0.50) plus about $0.59 in card processing, so you keep $7.91. On a Discover sale Gumroad takes 30%, $3.00, and you keep $7.00.

Does Gumroad charge payment processing on top of its fee?

Third-party summaries say yes for direct sales: about 2.9% + $0.30 on top of the 10% + $0.50. Discover's 30% is reported to include processing. Check Gumroad's pricing page, and edit the rates here if your payouts show something different.

Is Gumroad worth it for cheap products?

The fixed fees hurt. A $1 product sold directly keeps about $0.07 after fees. Below $4.68 you keep more from a Discover sale than a direct one, so for low prices, bundles work better.

Is it better to get sales from Discover or my own link?

Above $4.68, your own link keeps more per sale: $0.91 more at $10. Discover is still worth having as extra sales you wouldn't otherwise get.
