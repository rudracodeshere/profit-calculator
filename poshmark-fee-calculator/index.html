Poshmark's two-tier fee

Poshmark keeps one fee per sale. Under $15, it is a flat $2.95. At $15 or more, it is 20% of the sale price. The buyer pays for shipping with Poshmark's prepaid label, so shipping doesn't come out of your earnings and isn't charged a fee.

Earnings = price - $2.95 (under $15)  |  Earnings = price x 0.80 ($15 and up)

Example: a top you paid $8 for sells for $30. Poshmark takes $6.00 and you earn $24.00, so your profit is $16.00.

The $15 trap

Because the fee switches from flat to percentage, earnings drop when you cross the threshold. A $14.99 sale earns $12.04. A $15.00 sale earns $12.00. Every price from $15.00 to $15.04 earns less than $14.99 does, and you need to list at $15.06 to earn more ($12.05). If your item is priced in that band, take it down to $14.99.

The flat fee also makes cheap items costly to sell. On a $5 item, the $2.95 fee is 59.0% of the sale and you earn $2.05. Bundles help, since the fee is charged once per order.

Pricing backward from what you want to earn

Divide by 0.8 when the result lands at $15 or above, otherwise add $2.95. To earn $25 you list at $31.25. To earn $10 you list at $12.95. The calculator picks whichever rule gives the lower price, so it never sends you into the gap just above $15. Remember offers: buyers expect to negotiate, so list a little above the price you actually need.

Questions

How much does Poshmark take from a $30 sale?

20%, or $6.00, so you earn $24.00. Shipping is paid by the buyer and isn't counted.

Is it better to price at $14.99 or $15 on Poshmark?

$14.99. It earns $12.04 after the $2.95 flat fee, while $15.00 earns $12.00 after the 20% fee. You need $15.06 to earn more than you would at $14.99.

What should I list at to earn $25 on Poshmark?

$31.25. Poshmark takes 20%, $6.25, and you keep $25.00.

Does Poshmark charge fees on shipping?

No. The buyer pays for the prepaid shipping label and the fee is based on the sale price only.
