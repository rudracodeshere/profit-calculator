Two different fee shapes

Etsy takes a smaller percentage plus fixed amounts: 6.5% + 3% of the order, plus $0.25 processing and $0.20 listing, about 9.5% + $0.45. Amazon Handmade takes one bigger percentage, a 15% referral fee on the item and shipping, with a $1.00 minimum per item, and nothing fixed on top. Approved Handmade artisans don't pay the $39.99 monthly Professional plan fee that other Amazon sellers pay.

Because one has the higher rate and the other the fixed fee, the lines cross. On small orders, Etsy's fixed $0.45 weighs more than Amazon's extra 5.5 points. On anything bigger, Amazon's 15% costs more. Amazon's $1.00 minimum adds a second crossing at the very bottom.

Etsy = 9.5% x order + $0.45  |  Amazon Handmade = max(15% x order, $1.00)
Crossover: 15% x T = 9.5% x T + $0.45, so T = $0.45 / 5.5% = $8.18

A worked example

A $35 necklace with $5 shipping is a $40 order. Etsy takes $4.25; Amazon Handmade takes $6.00. With the same $6 label on both, Etsy saves you $1.75 an order, $35.00 a month at 20 orders.

The calculator solves the crossover with your numbers. With the defaults, Amazon is cheaper only between $5.79 and $8.18. A $6 order shows why: Etsy's fees are $1.02, Amazon's minimum is $1.00.

Where FBA changes the answer

If you send stock to Amazon's warehouses, the FBA fee replaces your own label and packing. When the FBA fee is lower than what you spend to ship an order yourself, Amazon can win on much larger orders. With a $5.00 FBA fee against your $6 label, Amazon is cheaper on every order under $26.36. Your FBA fee depends on the item's size and weight, so check it in Amazon's fee preview before you rely on this. FBA also adds storage and inbound shipping costs not shown here.

Beyond fees

Etsy shoppers come looking for handmade and personalized items; Amazon Handmade puts your work in front of people already shopping on Amazon, often with Prime delivery expectations. Amazon Handmade requires an application and approval as an artisan; Etsy lets anyone open a shop. Etsy also charges Offsite Ads on some orders, which this comparison leaves out; if many of your orders come from them, see the Offsite Ads calculator.

Questions

What are Amazon Handmade's fees?

A 15% referral fee on the item price plus shipping, with a minimum of $1.00 per item, and no monthly Professional plan fee for approved artisans. On a $40 order that is $6.00.

Is Amazon Handmade cheaper than Etsy?

Usually not on fees. On a $40 order Etsy takes $4.25 and Amazon Handmade $6.00. Amazon only wins on very small orders, or when FBA costs you less than shipping the order yourself.

Does Amazon Handmade have a monthly fee?

Not for approved Handmade artisans: the $39.99 Professional plan fee is waived. If it were charged, at 20 orders a month it would add about $2.00 per order, and Etsy would be cheaper at every order total.

Can I sell the same items on Etsy and Amazon Handmade?

Yes, as long as each item meets Amazon Handmade's rules for handmade goods. Selling on both means tracking stock and profit in two places, since each reports fees in its own format.
