How eBay fees work

Final value fee. eBay takes a percentage of the whole order: the item price, the shipping you charge and the sales tax the buyer pays. For most categories that is 13.6%. You don't keep the tax, but you still pay the fee on it.

Per-order fee. A fixed $0.40 on every order, or $0.30 if the order total is $10 or less.

Promoted listings. If you promote a listing at a chosen ad rate and the sale comes through the ad, eBay charges that rate on the same order total. A 5% ad rate costs you more than a third of the final value fee again.

Top Rated Plus. Top Rated sellers get 10% off the final value fee on listings that qualify (fast handling and free 30-day returns). It is a discount on the fee, not 10% of the sale.

The formula

eBay fees = (item + shipping + tax) x (FVF % + ad %) + per-order fee

Example: a $40 item with $8 shipping to a buyer who pays 8% sales tax. The buyer pays $51.84, of which $3.84 is tax. The final value fee is $7.05 and the per-order fee $0.40, so eBay keeps $7.45, which is 15.5% of the $48 you charged. After a $12 item cost and a $6 label you keep $22.55.

Promote the same listing at 5% and the ad adds $2.59, leaving $19.96. Without the ad but with Top Rated Plus, the final value fee drops by $0.71 and you keep $23.25.

Pricing tips

Because the fee is charged on shipping too, moving cost from the item price into the shipping charge does not save fees. Price your item so it still makes money after roughly 15% to 16% of the total goes to eBay, more if you promote. On small sales the fixed fee hurts most: an $8 item with free shipping pays $1.39 in fees, over 17% of the sale.

Questions

Does eBay charge fees on sales tax?

Yes. The final value fee is calculated on the total amount of the sale, which includes sales tax. On a $48 order with 8% tax, you pay the fee on $51.84.

How much does eBay take from a $40 sale?

With $8 shipping and 8% tax, $7.45 in total: $7.05 final value fee plus the $0.40 per-order fee, at the 13.6% rate. Your category rate may differ.

How is the promoted listing fee calculated?

It is your ad rate times the same total the final value fee uses, item plus shipping plus tax. A 5% ad rate on a $51.84 order costs $2.59, charged only when the sale is attributed to the ad.

What is the eBay per-order fee?

$0.40 per order, or $0.30 when the order total is $10 or less. On an $8 order with free shipping and no tax, fees come to $1.39 including the $0.30.
