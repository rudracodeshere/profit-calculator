Every Etsy fee, explained

Listing fee: $0.20. You pay it when you publish a listing and again each time an item sells and the listing auto-renews. A quantity-3 order costs $0.60 in listing fees.

Transaction fee: 6.5% of the order total: item price, the shipping you charge, and gift wrap. Offering free shipping doesn't avoid it. It just means the fee is charged on a higher item price.

Payment processing: 3% + $0.25 per order in the US, also on the order total. Other countries have their own rate.

Offsite Ads: 15% or 12%, only on orders that came from an Etsy ad on another site within 30 days of the click, capped at $100 per order. See the Offsite Ads calculator to check whether those sales still make money.

Currency conversion: 2.5% when your listings are priced in a different currency from your payment account.

The quick formula

Etsy fees (US) = 9.5% of (items + shipping) + $0.25 + $0.20 per item

Example: a $25 item with $5 shipping. The order total is $30, so the fees are $1.95 (transaction) + $1.15 (processing) + $0.20 (listing) = $3.30, or 11% of the order. With $8 of materials and a $5 label, you keep $13.70. If the same order came from Offsite Ads, another $4.50 goes, leaving $9.20.

Why the percentage is higher on cheap items

The $0.45 of fixed fees per order ($0.25 processing plus the $0.20 listing fee) barely matters on a $60 order, but on a $5 sticker it adds 9 points to the share Etsy takes. Bundling low-priced items, or setting a minimum order, keeps fixed fees from eating small sales.

Questions

How much does Etsy take from a $20 sale?

With free shipping, $2.35: $1.30 transaction fee, $0.85 processing and the $0.20 listing fee. That is 11.75% of the sale, before any Offsite Ads fee.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. Both the 6.5% transaction fee and the 3% processing fee apply to the shipping you charge the buyer.

Does Etsy charge fees on sales tax?

The 6.5% transaction fee is not charged on sales tax. In the US the 3% processing fee is calculated on the full amount the buyer pays, tax included, which adds a few cents per order. This calculator leaves tax out to keep the inputs simple.

Is Etsy Plus worth it?

Etsy Plus costs $10 a month and gives listing and ad credits. It only pays off if you would spend that on listings and Etsy Ads anyway; treat it as monthly overhead, not a per-sale fee.
