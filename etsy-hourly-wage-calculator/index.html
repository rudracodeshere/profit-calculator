How your real hourly wage is worked out

Everything left after Etsy's fees, materials and the label is what pays you. Divide it by all the time a sale takes, not only the making. Packing, printing the label, the post office run, photographing and writing the listing, and answering buyer messages are all part of the job. For listing time, take the hours you spent on photos and listings this month and divide by the items you sold.

Hourly wage = (price + shipping − Etsy fees − materials − label) ÷ hours per sale

To find the price that pays a target wage, the fees have to be solved for, because they grow with the price:

Price = (wage × hours + materials + label + $0.45) ÷ (1 − 9.5%) − shipping charged

Worked example

A $36 item with free shipping, $9 in materials and packaging, and a $5 label. Etsy takes $3.87, which leaves $18.13. It takes 60 minutes to make, 10 to pack and 10 of listing and admin, 80 minutes in total. That is $13.60 an hour, under a $15 minimum wage.

Counting only the hour of making, it looks like $18.13 an hour. The 20 minutes of packing and admin are what pull it under minimum wage.

To earn $20 an hour the price has to be $45.43. To match a $15 minimum wage it needs $38.07. At $50 the wage would be $23.10.

What to do if the number is low

Raise the price first. In the example, going from $36 to $45.43 takes the wage from $13.60 to $20. Testing a higher price on a few listings costs you nothing but a few weeks.

Cut time that isn't making. Batch your photography, reuse listing templates, buy labels online in one session. Time you save on admin raises your hourly wage without touching the price.

Make in batches. Twenty of an item rarely take twenty times as long as one. Time a batch and divide by the count for a truer minutes-to-make.

The minutes per item vary across a catalog, and so does the wage. Matching your sales export to time per product shows which listings are paying you and which are a hobby.

Questions

How do I calculate my hourly wage selling on Etsy?

Subtract Etsy's fees, materials and the shipping label from what the buyer pays, then divide by the hours one sale takes, including packing and listing time. The $36 example leaves $18.13 for 80 minutes, which is $13.60 an hour.

Should I include listing and photography time?

Yes. It is real work the sale depends on. Spread it across your sales: if you spend 5 hours a month on photos and listings and sell 30 items, that is 10 minutes per sale.

What should I charge to pay myself $20 an hour on Etsy?

Add your wage for the time, materials, label and $0.45 of fixed fees, then divide by 0.905 to cover the 9.5% Etsy takes. For 80 minutes of work with $14 of costs, that is $45.43.

Is it normal to earn less than minimum wage on Etsy?

Many makers do, often because they count only making time. If your number is under minimum wage, the calculator shows the price that fixes it: $38.07 for the example to reach $15 an hour.
